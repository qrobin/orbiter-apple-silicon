# Earth imagery

`earth-8k.jpg` is an 8192 × 4096 resampling of NASA's Blue Marble: Next Generation, May 2004, topography composite.

Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography/
Original image: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography/may/world.topo.200405.3x21600x10800.jpg
Credit: NASA Earth Observatory, Reto Stöckli and the Blue Marble team.

This is a monthly visual composite, not current satellite weather or elevation geometry. At this resolution one pixel represents about 4.9 km at the equator. The original 21600 × 10800 JPEG is downloaded to the developer cache, not redistributed here.

Earth cloud coverage: an 8192 × 4096 snapshot of Matt Eason's Live Cloud Maps.
https://github.com/matteason/live-cloud-maps
https://clouds.matteason.co.uk/images/8192x4096/clouds.jpg
Source snapshot retrieved 2026-10-29; SHA-256 of the JPEG:
d871cc4615e32a2116c40c0c02aec05257224a8f3e95a420a27823f27ef8dfb5
Contains modified EUMETSAT data. Project code and images are released under
CC0 1.0, subject to the EUMETSAT attribution above. The bundled map is a fixed
snapshot, not live weather; polar areas are filled by mirrored source data.
Converted to grayscale and reduced for earth-clouds.r8 with
tools/prepare_cloud_snapshot.py.

Earth ocean mask: inverted alpha channel from NASA's Scientific Visualization
Studio Blue Marble land layer (SVS 4439), resampled to 4096 × 2048.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4439/
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004400/a004439/flat_bluemarble_8192x4096.tif
Generated with tools/extract_ocean_mask.py. Water surface detail is procedural;
earth-waves.png and earth-waves-detail.png are generated locally by
tools/generate_ocean_spectrum.py.
