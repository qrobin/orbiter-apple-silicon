# Planetary maps

The Sun, Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Saturn-ring maps are by **Solar System Scope / INOVE**, used under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.

Source and downloads: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The files are the original 2K downloads, renamed without the `2k_` prefix. The ring image is renamed `saturn-ring.png`. These are artist-adjusted maps based on NASA spacecraft imagery/elevation; the publisher notes that some unmapped areas are filled and colors are enhanced. They are visual assets, not scientific measurements. Earth retains the separately credited NASA 8K image.

Other moons and Vesta currently use procedural materials, with no claim that the generated markings depict actual geography.
